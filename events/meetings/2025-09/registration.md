This summer school is financed by [SFB-TRR 195](https://www.computeralgebra.de/sfb/), and
primarily intended for its PhD students. Outside applications by PhD students and other
young researchers are also welcome, but internal applications have precedence.

- Deadline for applications: August 15, 2025.

A notification of acceptance/non-acceptance will be issued to the applicants shortly
after the deadline.

To apply, please send an email with the following information to
[Barbara Ermisch](mailto:ermisch@mathematik.uni-kl.de):
  - Name
  - Affiliation
  - Name of Adviser
  - Topic of PhD (if applicable)
  - Topic of Master Thesis
  - Research Interests
  - Quantify your experience (none/beginner/intermediate/advanced) in ...
    - using git
    - programming in general
    - programming in Julia
    - using OSCAR
